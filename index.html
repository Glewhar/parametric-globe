Get the 3D model

A parametric 3D Earth built from real data. A biome world map was redrawn with python as an SVG, all curves were corrected to be watertight and non-overlapping. NASA satellite imagery and topography rasters were then sampled to enrich each landmass with accurate elevation and biome coloring for all seasons. Every curve of the svg was Bézier-projected onto a CAD sphere and lofted from the sphere's center. all ~450 bodies are watertight and clean
